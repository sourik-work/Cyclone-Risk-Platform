- **Resolution:** 1080p (1920x1080) at 60fps
- **Audio:** Crisp microphone narration with control-room dark theme backdrop

## 🎬 Section-by-Section Cue Sheet

1. **[0:00 - 0:30] Introduction & Problem Framing**
   - *Screen:* Fullscreen view of the live dark-mode platform interface at `https://cyclone-risk-platform.vercel.app`.
   - *Narration:* "Welcome to the Cyclone Risk & Anticipatory Action Platform. The Bay of Bengal experiences only 6% of the world's cyclones, yet over 50% of global cyclone fatalities occur along these coastal districts. While meteorology has improved, the critical bottleneck is the operational lead-time gap between warning and anticipatory action. Our platform solves this."

2. **[0:30 - 1:30] Historical Replay & Model Validation Card**
   - *Screen:* Click "Historical Cyclone Fani (2019)", zoom in on Puri landfall track. Open the **Model Validation Card**.
   - *Narration:* "Notice our TrackLSTM forecasting model. Rather than making unsubstantiated claims of beating national agencies, we have reframed TrackLSTM as a complementary ensemble member with published 18-storm Leave-One-Storm-Out cross-validation. At 24 hours, our mean RMSE is 79.5 km with a 95% bootstrap confidence interval between 75.6 and 83.6 km, compared to a persistence baseline of 112.4 km."

3. **[1:30 - 2:30] Live Data Freshness & APAC Agency Adapters**
   - *Screen:* Switch to "Live Monitoring". Hover over the Freshness Badge and open the APAC Agency Status modal.
   - *Narration:* "In live mode, our ingestion layer monitors data staleness in real-time with automated circuit breakers that trip on upstream failures. Our modular architecture features live scrapers and WMO parsers for IMD, the Philippines' PAGASA, and the Joint Typhoon Warning Center, proving basin-wide portability across the Asia-Pacific."

4. **[2:30 - 3:30] Gemini 3.7 Flash Exposure Reasoning & 95% Uncertainty Cone**
   - *Screen:* Click on Puri district; expand the Gemini Exposure Reasoning panel. Show the yellow uncertainty cone on the map.
   - *Narration:* "Here, Gemini 3.7 Flash reasons over coastal infrastructure exposure. Crucially, we inject the validated 95% forecast uncertainty cone—155.9 km radius at 24 hours—directly into Gemini's context window. Gemini avoids false precision, assigning probabilistic exposure confidence to each power grid substation and hospital."

5. **[3:30 - 4:30] Calibrated Parametric Insurance & Human-in-the-Loop Gate**
   - *Screen:* Open the Parametric Insurance Trigger Panel. Click "Review & Approve".
   - *Narration:* "Our parametric insurance module is calibrated against real Indian state risk schemes, including Kerala SDMA and Nagaland DRTPS. For a major storm like Fani, the model computes ₹58.8 Crores in pre-landfall liquidity, while a sub-threshold depression strictly returns zero. In compliance with disaster protocols, payouts and advisories require human sign-off. Our state machine captures operator review latency and confidence."

6. **[4:30 - 5:00] Multi-Channel Dispatch & Immutable Audit Trail**
   - *Screen:* Submit approval feedback and observe parallel dispatch metrics.
   - *Narration:* "Upon approval, the platform executes a parallel multi-channel fanout—dispatching DLT-compliant SMS, outbound IVR voice calls in 11 vernaculars generated by Gemini TTS, and localized Community Radio queues. Every transition is logged immutably to Firestore. Thank you."
